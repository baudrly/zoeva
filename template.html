CLOP2 · six genomes · held-out chromosomes

Which organism, and which kind of sequence, from 500 bases alone

CLOP2 is a small DNA–text contrastive encoder trained on 44 species × sequence-class cells, from E. coli to human. Zoeva reads a window through it, votes among annotated neighbours, and ranks species and Sequence Ontology classes. Everything on this page runs in your browser, on windows from chromosomes the model never saw.

Zoeva · automatic hypotheses

Hypotheses generated, registered and tested

Necessity: drop in Zoeva's class probability when the motif's occurrences are randomised, beyond the drop from randomising as many 6 bp blocks elsewhere in the same windows (validation · test). Enrichment: odds ratio of carrying the seed, held-out windows of the cell against the species' other classes. Sufficient: implanting the seed into other windows raises the class probability more than a letter-shuffled seed, on both splits. Click a row to read a held-out window that carries it.
